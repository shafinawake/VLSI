# VLSI Design & Engineering Portfolio

Welcome to my **VLSI (Very Large-Scale Integration) repository** — a collection of academic projects, coursework, technical experiments, and engineering work related to digital hardware design and integrated circuits.

This repository documents my learning journey in VLSI, bringing together project reports, design work, and practical knowledge gained during and beyond my coursework.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Strengthen my understanding of VLSI design principles and digital systems.
- Apply theoretical concepts to practical hardware design problems.
- Document academic projects, experiments, and engineering progress.
- Build a portfolio of VLSI-related work for future research and development.
